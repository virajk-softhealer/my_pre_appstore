Freshdesk Connector

The Freshdesk Odoo Connector provides a seamless two-way synchronization of contacts and tickets between Freshdesk and Odoo. By integrating your support data flow, this app allows you to manage both platforms efficiently. Enjoy manual and automated sync options, streamline customer service operations, and ensure that your helpdesk agents and Odoo users are always working with the latest information.

Key Features

Features

Freshdesk Configuration

Easily configure Freshdesk API credentials and sync settings in Odoo.

Two-way Contact Sync

Seamlessly sync contacts between Odoo and Freshdesk in real-time or via scheduled actions.

Two-way Ticket Sync

Manage helpdesk tickets efficiently by syncing them across both platforms.

Queue Management

Track and manage synchronization operations through a robust queue system.

Webhook Integration

Utilize webhooks for instant data synchronization from Freshdesk to Odoo.

Navigate to Settings > Users & Companies > Users and grant the 'User' or 'Manager' access rights for Freshdesk to the corresponding users.


Navigate to the Integration Hub and view the Overview dashboard to check the initial Disconnected status of the Freshdesk Connector.


Navigate to Freshdesk > Settings in the Integration Hub to access the Authentication and Sync configuration options.


Locate and copy your Freshdesk API Key from your Freshdesk Profile Settings.


Enter your Freshdesk Domain and API Key in the Authentication settings, then click 'Build Connection' to establish a successful connection.


Once successfully connected, the Integration Hub Overview dashboard will display the Freshdesk Connector status as 'Connected'.


In Odoo, you can update a contact's details, such as their address, and click the 'Sync to Freshdesk' button. A success message confirms the sync.


The Integration Logs will record an 'Update' operation with a 'Success' status for the contact sync.


You can view the detailed JSON payload of the synchronization in the Integration Logs, which includes the updated fields.


In Freshdesk, verify that the contact's details, such as the highlighted address, have been successfully updated.


In the settings, ensure that 'Create', 'Update', and 'Delete' are enabled under 'Contact Sync'.


To sync a new contact from Odoo to Freshdesk, click the 'Sync to Freshdesk' button on the contact form. A success message will appear and the Freshdesk Details tab will be populated.


The Integration Logs will record a 'Create' operation with a 'Success' status, confirming the contact was created in Freshdesk.


In Freshdesk, verify that the new contact's details have been successfully synced and are visible.


You can also sync internal notes. Add your notes in the 'Notes' tab of the Odoo contact.


The Integration Logs will record an 'Update' operation reflecting the note synchronization.


The synced notes will be visible in the 'About' section of the contact's details in Freshdesk.


To delete a synced contact, use the Action menu in Odoo and select Delete. Confirm the deletion in the popup wizard.


The Integration Logs will record a 'Delete' operation with a 'Success' status, and a notification confirms the contact was removed from Freshdesk.


In Freshdesk, navigate to the 'Deleted contacts' view to confirm that the contact has been successfully moved to the deleted list.


In the Settings, under 'Sync From Odoo To Freshdesk', enable Create, Update, and Delete options for 'Ticket Sync'.


In Odoo Helpdesk, create or open a ticket. Make sure a customer is assigned.


You can view the related tickets for a contact in Odoo using the Tickets smart button.


Click on the 'Sync to Freshdesk' button on the ticket. A success message will appear once synced.


Navigate to Integration Hub > Integration Logs to view the successful 'Create' operation for the Helpdesk Ticket.


In Freshdesk, go to the Tickets view to see the newly synced ticket from Odoo.


In Freshdesk, you can also see the related ticket under the Tickets tab of the corresponding contact.


Update the ticket details in Odoo, such as the description, and click 'Sync to Freshdesk' again. A success message will confirm the sync.


The Integration Logs will record an 'Update' operation with a 'Success' status for the ticket.


In Freshdesk, open the ticket to confirm that the details have been successfully updated.


If you delete the ticket in Odoo, the Integration Logs will record a 'Delete' operation with a 'Success' status.


In Freshdesk, navigate to the 'Trash' view to confirm that the ticket has been successfully deleted.


In Freshdesk, create or update a contact or ticket to test the sync.


In Odoo, navigate to the Freshdesk sync operations menu.


Trigger the 'Fetch from Freshdesk' operation manually.


Odoo will fetch the newly created or updated records from Freshdesk.


Verify the imported contacts in the Odoo Contacts app.


The Freshdesk ID is stored against the Odoo partner for future updates.


Verify the imported tickets in the Odoo Helpdesk app.


The tickets will have the corresponding Freshdesk information mapped.


Sync logs will record the successful import operations.


You can schedule the automatic fetch operation using the Odoo scheduled actions.


Sync Freshdesk to Odoo.


Sync Freshdesk to Odoo scheduled action.


Navigate to Integration Hub > Freshdesk Settings and enable the "Enable Sync Queue" option under the Queue Management section.


The Integration Hub Overview dashboard will now display the number of pending items in the Sync Queue, initially showing 0.


Create or update a record in Odoo, such as a Helpdesk Ticket named "Ticket Demo 202".


The Integration Hub Overview dashboard will now reflect the newly added records in the Sync Queue count, such as 2 pending items.


Navigate to Integration Hub > Integration Queue to view the list of pending synchronization tasks, displaying their Model, Contact, Ticket, Direction, Operation, and Status.


Click on a specific queue item to view its details, showing the Pending status and record information.


Navigate to Settings > Technical > Scheduled Actions and select the "Freshdesk: Process Sync Queue" action. Click "Run Manually" to process the queue.


Once processed, the queue item's status will update to "Done".


The Integration Queue list will now display the processed records with a "Success" status.


You can view the detailed JSON request and response data under the "Synchronization Details" tab for a Contact record.


Similarly, you can view the synchronization details JSON for a Helpdesk Ticket record.


On the Contact form, the "Freshdesk Details" tab will now populate with the corresponding "Freshdesk ID" and "Last Synced (FD)" timestamp.


On the Helpdesk Ticket form, the "Freshdesk Details" tab will display the "Freshdesk Ticket ID", "Last Synced (FD)" timestamp, along with Freshdesk Status and Priority.


In your Freshdesk portal, the synchronized Contact will appear under the Contacts section with the exported details.


Similarly, the synchronized Ticket will appear under the Tickets section in Freshdesk, linked to the corresponding contact.


Navigate to Integration Hub > Freshdesk Settings and enable the "Enable Webhook" option to receive real-time updates from Freshdesk.


In Freshdesk, navigate to Admin > Workflows > Automations.


Under the Automations settings, go to the "Ticket Creation" tab to configure rules for new tickets.


Under the Automations settings, go to the "Ticket Updates" tab to configure rules for updating tickets.


Back in Odoo, under the Freshdesk Webhook settings, select a Default Helpdesk Team and select Ticket Events for Create and Update.


In Freshdesk, create a new contact (e.g., Bob Tree) and provide the necessary contact information.


In Freshdesk, create a new ticket associated with the contact (e.g., Ticket Demo 201).


Navigate to the Integration Hub Overview dashboard to see the connected status and synchronization counts for Contacts and Tickets.


View the updated Sync Logs and Sync Queue counts in the Integration Hub Overview to verify synchronization operations.


Navigate to Integration Logs to view the synchronization details and JSON payload for the created Contact.


Navigate to Integration Logs to view the synchronization details and JSON payload for the created Ticket.


In Odoo, open the synced Contact to view the associated Tickets and verify the Freshdesk Details tab with the synced ID.


In Odoo, open the synced Helpdesk Ticket to verify the Customer assignment and the Freshdesk Details tab with synced status and ID.
